# J2 2×4 → 2×6 for the universal harness (2026-11-09)

**Why.** T's direction: canode lives at each vehicle's CAN gateway. One board, one universal pigtail,
then a T-harness per vehicle. The board connector needs enough inputs for that. T chose to grow J2
in rev A, before ordering (bypass relay and saved feature profiles are rev B).

## What changed

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| J2 | Molex 43045-0812 (2×4) | **Molex 43045-1212 (2×6)**, same position; pin 1 unchanged, the header grows 6 mm toward the board edge |
| J2 pinout | 1 12V · 2 CAN1H · 3 CAN2H · 4 AUX_IN / 5 GND · 6 CAN1L · 7 CAN2L · 8 AUX_OUT | 1 12V · 2 CAN1H · 3 CAN2H · 4 AUX_IN · **5 IGN_IN · 6 AUX_IN2** / 7 GND · 8 CAN1L · 9 CAN2L · 10 AUX_OUT · **11 HARNESS_ID · 12 GND** |
| Mating plug | 43025-0800 | 43025-1200 |

- **New parts**, all JLC Basic and already on the BOM:

  | Refs | Parts | Function |
  |---|---|---|
  | R19, R20, C18 | 100k, 10k, 100 nF | IGN_IN divider, the same 0–36 V divider as VBAT/AUX_IN |
  | R21, R22, C19 | 100k, 10k, 100 nF | AUX_IN2 divider, the same 0–36 V divider |
  | R23, R24, C20 | 10k pull-up, 10k series, 100 nF | HARNESS_ID: the T-harness's resistor to pin 12 sets the ADC reading |

- **MCU pins** (`make pins` checks them against ST's pin data):
  - IGN_SENSE → PB10 (ADC1_IN11)
  - AUX2_SENSE → PB11 (ADC1_IN15)
  - ID_SENSE → PB12 (ADC1_IN16)

  These are U1's last three bottom-row pins. PA4/PA7 were tried first, but their escape routes were boxed in on both layers.
- **Why dividers on all three inputs:** any vehicle voltage (24 V jump, 35 V suppressed dump) is safe on them, and they use only resistors and capacitors already on the board. Ignition doesn't need its own wake source, because CAN traffic already wakes the board.
- **HARNESS_ID fault case:** 12–36 V on pin 11 pushes at most ~3 mA through 10 kΩ into the pin clamp and the 3.3 V rail.

## Board
- J2 swapped in place. The old pads 1–4 / 5–8 are now 1–4 / 7–10 at the same coordinates (asserted in the script), so their routing was kept.
- D7 moved to (103.5, 138.0) and R18 to (103.5, 145.0), both rotated 90°, to clear the longer header.
- The nine new 0603s sit in the open area right of U1, at x 162–170 mm and y 111–115 mm.
- Re-route, in several Freerouting 2.1 passes (all other copper locked, only new wiring imported):
  1. the AUX_IN / AUX_OUT / AUX_FLY nets around the moved parts, plus the new nets;
  2. the signal nets around U1's bottom-right corner, freed so the PB10–PB12 escapes could get out (CAN2, UART, LEDs, SWD, USB_DM, AUX_DRV, GPIO_PB9 were re-routed there);
  3. LED1/LED2/UART_TX/UART_RX together.
- Hand-routed:
  - VBATF to D7 and R11, by a via beside F1, a 0.5 mm track on B.Cu along the left board edge, and a branch along y 133.5 to R11;
  - NRST from U1 pin 10 down the channel between the HSE_OUT and VBAT_SENSE traces;
  - ID_SENSE from R24 through a via and B.Cu, to cross the +3V3 track that runs between C20's pads;
  - GND vias at C19, C20 and R22, whose ground pads the router had boxed in.
- Freerouting notes:
  - `-mp` is ignored by 2.1. Use `--router.max_passes=N`.
  - One run that ran to its own end ("restoring an earlier board") wrote an incomplete `.ses`. Capping the passes avoided it.
- Back-silkscreen pinout rewritten for 12 pins and moved below the header.
- J2 3D model: new stand-ins `hardware/3dmodels/Molex_Micro-Fit_3.0_43045-1212_…` and `…43025-1200_plug_mated.step`.

## New check: pad-net parity
`kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity` checks footprints and fields, **not pad nets**. During this change, U1's three ADC pads were still on their old `unconnected-(…)` nets, and DRC reported "0 parity issues". `validation/check_netparity.py` compares every board pad's net with the schematic netlist. It now runs in `make drc`.
