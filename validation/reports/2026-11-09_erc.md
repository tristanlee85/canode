## Warnings left as-is: `lib_symbol_mismatch` (11)
The schematic embeds the KiCad 7 copies of its library symbols; KiCad 10's libraries have since changed.
Pin numbers and electrical types were compared pin by pin (embedded vs. KiCad 10 library):

| Symbol (refs) | Pin numbers / types | What differs in KiCad 10 |
|---|---|---|
| AO3400A (Q1), LED (D4–D6), NUP2105L (U4, U6) | identical | graphics/properties only |
| STM32G0B1C_B-C-E_Tx (U1) | identical | symbol widened: **every pin moved**; pins 33/34 renamed `PA11/PA9`→`PA9/PA11`, `PA12/PA10`→`PA10/PA12`; alternate-function lists renamed |
| Crystal_GND24 (Y1) | identical | GND pins renamed `2`/`4`→`G`; **pin 2 moved** from top to bottom |
| SolderJumper_2_Open (JP1, JP2) | identical | **pins moved** (±3.81 → ±5.08 mm) |
| USB_C_Receptacle_USB2.0_16P (J1) | shield pin **`S1` → `SH`** | would no longer match the footprint's shield pad |

**Electrically the design is unaffected**: the embedded copies are what ERC, the netlist and the PCB use.

**Do not run *Tools → Update Symbols from Library* on U1, Y1, JP1/JP2 or J1.** This schematic connects
pins with label stubs placed at the old pin endpoints; moved pins would leave the stubs dangling and
silently disconnect those nets (all of U1), and J1's shield would lose its pad mapping.
If the warnings become noise, the clean fix is to copy these symbols into the project library
(`canode.kicad_sym`) and point the schematic at them. Not done yet; it's a T decision.

## Changes made
- `validation/check_pins.py`: KiCad 10 netlists append `_<pin number>` to each pin's `pinfunction`
  (e.g. `PA0_11`), which made all 44 name comparisons fail. The loader now strips that suffix; older
  netlists without it are unaffected. No schematic or board files changed.
